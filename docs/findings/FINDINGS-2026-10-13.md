# Findings after deployment (2026-10-13) - source of truth for wave 2
Evidence: live site pages, Level 2 test result (screenshots) and the generated PDF report.

## Website
- W1 Privacy page publishes an internal draft banner ("Návrh textu na schválenie vlastníkom a právnym poradcom.") and an internal config statement about retention and deletion not being applied.
- W2 Processors: Web3Forms and FormSubmit relay audit/contact data; Resend stores account data (logs, metadata) in the US even with an EU sending region; Supabase region and Edge Function region not documented; policy has no third-country section.
- W3 `/self-audit/` and `/o-nas/`: og:title, og:url, og:description and Twitter tags are copied from the homepage (canonical is correct). `<title>` shows the brand twice ("O nás | AVENG | AVENG").
- W4 Thin pages: pillar pages (~70 words), `/optimalizacia-a-ai/`, `/o-nas/` (~100 words, no deliverables, process, engagement model or FAQ).
- W5 Inconsistent claims: "odvetvový benchmark" / "odvetvovému štandardu" vs. internal expert-estimate references; level names Silver/Gold vs Premium.
- W6 Developer phrasing in public copy ("ak je na webe nastavené", "služby, ktoré web skutočne používa").
- W7 Full contact block repeated in the footer of every page, including `/kontakt/`.
- W8 Privacy notice repeated three times on `/self-audit/`.
- W9 Seat shown as "Košeca 01864" (no street/number) - owner to verify against the commercial register.
- W10 Identical meta keywords on all pages (no value).
- W11 FAQ retention answer is vague.
- W12 Buttons "Dohodnúť osobnú schôdzku" / "Požiadať o prístupový kód" are not clear links; booking URL not set.
- W13 Search engines still show an old cached homepage (owner action).

## Audit result and report (Level 2 test: revenue 500 000 EUR, COGS 65 %)
- A1 Level 1 wording shown on a Level 2 result and PDF (disclaimer, hint "or move to Level 2", PDF footer "Indikatívny skríning").
- A2 Per-domain amounts shown to the euro on the web (24 944 EUR) while the headline and PDF are rounded.
- A3 Point estimate shown together with reliability "nízka" (no range).
- A4 The cost-base coefficients (k_Pi as share of COGS: 25/15/15/20/10 %, total 85 %) are not disclosed to the reader.
- A5 PDF lacks the "internal expert estimate" sentence and the methodology; still says "voči odvetvovému štandardu".
- A6 Level 2 (290 EUR) shows the same five-domain table as Level 1; metric explanations are generic definitions; no per-question result, no OEE/COPQ breakdown.
- A7 Metric explainers repeated up to three times per domain.
- A8 Recommendations look templated per domain, not derived from the weakest answers.
- A9 Impact labels do not follow the euro gap (D1 8.3k = "vysoký", D4 19.9k = "stredný").
- A10 After Level 2 the Level 2 unlock card is still shown; ordering/payment path unclear.
- A11 Decimal point instead of comma ("56.5 %"); English "(Plastics & Rubber)"; awkward sentence "Celok je súčet procesov."
- A12 Column "efektivita %" is a maturity score, but reads like a measured KPI (D4 "Logistika/OTD 49,2 %").
- A13 Questionable methodology tags: OTD -> "Lean – lead time"; control plan -> "ISO 9001"; absence -> "ISO 45001".
- A14 PDF is dark-themed (poor for printing); CTA is a plain URL instead of e-mail/booking.
- A15 KPI comparison table not verified (needs a run with exact numbers).
